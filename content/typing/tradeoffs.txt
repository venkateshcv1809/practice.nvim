We choose Zig on the bootloader because the image must stay small and the
control flow must stay visible. We accept a smaller hiring pool.

We choose Rust on the application MCU because we cannot afford use-after-free
in the field. We accept longer compile times and more ceremony.

We choose MQTT over HTTP for telemetry because radios drop and sleep.
We accept a broker as a production dependency.

We choose QoS 1, not 2. Duplicates are cheaper than round trips.
We accept idempotent consumers as a requirement.

We choose two replicas, not ten. We do not know the load yet.
We accept that a node loss is visible. We will scale from a measurement.

We choose a managed broker for year one. We do not staff an MQTT team.
We accept vendor limits and a bill. We keep the topic layout portable.

A tradeoff card has four lines:
we choose X
because Y
we accept Z
we will revisit when W
If you cannot fill Z, you have not chosen. You have hoped.
