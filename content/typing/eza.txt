eza is a modern ls. It lists files, then gets out of the way.

eza
eza -l
eza -la
eza -l --git
eza --tree
eza --tree --level=2
eza --icons
eza -l --time-style=long-iso
eza -l --sort=modified
eza -l --group-directories-first
eza src lua tests

Compared with ls:
ls -la
eza -la --git

Useful aliases in ~/.zshrc:
alias ls='eza'
alias ll='eza -l --git --group-directories-first'
alias lt='eza --tree --level=2'

Pipe it like any other command:
eza -1 | wc -l
eza -1 | fzf
eza --json
