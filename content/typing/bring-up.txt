Bring-up is the first honest conversation with a board. Do not write an app yet.

1. Power. Measure 3V3 and 1V8 before you plug USB. Smell is a valid sensor.
2. Ground. Common ground with the probe. Floating grounds invent ghosts.
3. Reset. Hold reset, release, watch the current jump. No jump, no life.
4. Clocks. HSE / LSE / PLL. If the clock is wrong, everything is "almost".
5. Debug. SWD / JTAG. Connect, halt, read PC. If you cannot halt, stop.
6. Blink. One GPIO, one LED, one delay. This is the handshake.
7. UART. 115200 8N1. Print a line from reset. If you have logs, you have a job.
8. Flash. Read back the image you think you wrote.
9. Sensors. One bus, one ACK. Then the real driver.
10. Radio last. Radios hide power and timing bugs until you are tired.

Do not skip a step because the previous one "probably" worked.
Write the measurements down. Voltage, current, clock, probe serial.
The next failure will not remember your optimism.
