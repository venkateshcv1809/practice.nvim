A unit is a file. Read it before you restart production:

[Unit]
Description=site api
After=network.target

[Service]
Type=simple
User=app
WorkingDirectory=/srv/app
ExecStart=/usr/bin/app --config /etc/app.toml
Restart=on-failure
RestartSec=2

[Install]
WantedBy=multi-user.target

If the box is "down", this is the order:
systemctl --failed
journalctl -xe
journalctl -u that.service -n 200
Do not reboot to hide a crash loop.
