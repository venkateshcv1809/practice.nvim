xxh is SSH that can bring your shell with you. The remote machine stays
clean; your prompt, aliases, and plugins travel for the session.

xxh user@host
xxh user@host +s zsh
xxh user@host +s bash
xxh user@host +i
xxh +I xxh-plugin-zsh-ohmyzsh
xxh +L

Compared with plain ssh:
ssh user@host
# you get their shell, their PATH, their empty prompt
xxh user@host
# you get a familiar shell without writing files into their home

Common shape:
xxh ada@192.168.1.20 +s zsh
xxh ada@host -p 2222
xxh ada@host --install-xxh-home /tmp/xxh

If xxh is not installed yet, the idea is still worth typing:
ssh first to reach the machine.
xxh later, when you want your own keyboard habits on that machine.
Keep secrets out of the command line. Use keys, not passwords in history.
