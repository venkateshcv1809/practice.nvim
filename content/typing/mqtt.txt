MQTT is a small pub/sub protocol. Devices talk to a broker, not to each other.

mosquitto_sub -h localhost -t 'site/+/temp' -v
mosquitto_pub -h localhost -t 'site/a1/temp' -m '21.5' -q 1
mosquitto_pub -t 'site/a1/status' -m 'online' -r

Topics are paths, not folders:
site/{device_id}/temp
site/{device_id}/cmd
$SYS/# is for the broker. Do not publish there.

QoS:
0  at most once   fire and forget
1  at least once  you may see duplicates
2  exactly once   expensive; rarely your first choice

A device loop:
connect
subscribe site/{id}/cmd
publish site/{id}/status online (retained)
loop: read sensor, publish temp, wait 10s
on disconnect: retry with backoff, do not spin

Keep payloads small and boring:
{"t":21.5,"v":1}
Not a novel, not a stack dump, not a secret.

TLS and a unique password per device. One leaked firmware image should not
open the whole fleet.
