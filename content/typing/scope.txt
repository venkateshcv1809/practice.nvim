In scope:
bring-up of one MCU board to blink, UART, and MQTT telemetry
a retained online status and a temperature topic
a dashboard that shows last-seen and last value
a written ADR for the broker choice
weekly demo on hardware we both can power

Out of scope:
mobile apps
multi-tenant billing
custom plastic enclosure
certification (CE / FCC) until a separate SOW
24/7 on-call
source code escrow
a second radio stack

Change requests are new scope. They are quoted before they are built.
Hardware NRE and software time are billed apart. Unused hours do not
convert into extra boards.

Success is: a device we can reset, watch on the broker, and halt.
Not: a platform. Not: a brand. Not: "while you are in there".

If it is not in this list, it is a conversation, not a promise.
