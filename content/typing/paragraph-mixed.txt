Speed is a side effect. Accuracy is the path. If you chase 120 wpm while
dropping 12% of the glyphs, you are rehearsing chaos. Aim first for a clean
98% on a 45-wpm pass, then 55, then 70. The clock is allowed to wait.

Type like you are sending a patch, not winning a race. Paths such as
~/src/app/v2/main.ts and URLs like https://127.0.0.1:8080/health?ready=1
should feel ordinary. So should digits in dates (2026-10-31), money ($48.50),
and counts (3 windows, 11 buffers, 256 KiB).

Keep your eyes ~8-12 characters ahead. Do not stare at the key you just missed.
A single backspace is cheaper than a panicked burst of 4-7 corrections. When a
symbol cluster appears -- { } [ ] ( ) < > -- slow to 80% of your comfort pace,
then resume. Rhythm beats adrenaline.

A practical drill: 5 minutes of prose, 3 minutes of code, 2 minutes of numbers.
Log the result as 5+3+2, not as a mood. Tomorrow copy the same mix. In 14 days
you will have 14 quiet data points, not 14 arguments with yourself. Faster is
what accurate looks like after it has been repeated.
